New engine: the Go torrent engine is replaced by one written in Rust. Seeks fetch the new position first, move late blocks to faster peers and fetch MP4 and MKV keyframes ahead; the on-device peer model is gone. Saved downloads have their own queue and keep their speed. Less idle work, so the phone stays cooler. Recovers when switching between Wi-Fi and mobile data. Links from other apps open in Torrent Player's own window. Full notes: GitHub releases.
